Privacy Policy — TWT TikTok Bot

Owner: Paul Lattimore III (The Worst Traders) · Effective: November 20, 2026

TWT TikTok Bot accesses only the owner's own TikTok account (@worst.traders) through OAuth authorization (video.upload scope). Its access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own system.

The tool collects no data from any other person. It does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Nothing is retained beyond the authorization tokens needed to operate the tool, and the owner can revoke access at any time from his TikTok settings.
